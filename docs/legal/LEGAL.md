## Privacy notice

**In short: Planterra collects no personal data about you, and has no server that stores your plans.**

- **What is stored, and where?** Your plans, settings, uploaded photos and visualisations stay only on your own device, in the browser’s storage (IndexedDB, localStorage). They leave it only when you save them to a file or share them.
- **Shared link.** The read-only link carries the chosen variants (area names, plants, notes) compressed at the end of the link. Anyone who gets the link can see them. Its content is not stored on a server, but it can be forwarded anywhere, so only send it to people you want to share with.
- **Plant photos from Wikipedia.** With photos turned on (*View → Plant photos from Wikipedia*), the app asks the Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons servers using the plants’ Latin names. The Wikimedia Foundation then sees your IP address and the request as usual (see the [Wikimedia privacy policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy)). The photos are then stored on your device. The feature can be turned off.
- **Visualisation (ChatGPT).** You send the description and the plan yourself, from the share sheet or the clipboard. The chosen service’s data handling applies to what you give it.
- **Updates.** The app downloads its new versions from its own address. No data about you is sent for this.
- **Cookies, analytics, ads.** None.
- **Deletion.** Clearing the browser’s data, or the “Start over” button after an error, removes everything from the device.
- **Contact.** Questions about data handling can be addressed to the author, Kiss-Mihály István.

## Data sources

- **Plant data:** a starting list compiled from the K-07 and K-08 working documents and extended in the app. Values marked “to be checked” have not been reviewed by an expert yet; *Plant database → List of values to check* collects them.
- **Prices and pack sizes:** from the K-08 working document, estimated and for information only; those marked “shop” come from a specific offer.
- **Care calendar:** general gardening practice, adjusted to the climate set for the project.
- **Photos:** Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons, with the author, licence and source shown under each photo; or your own upload.
